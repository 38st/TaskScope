# TaskScope Brand Direction

## Name

TaskScope is broad enough for a process monitor while remaining focused and
utility-like. It does not tie the app to GlobalProtect or any single process
group.

## Visual system

- Rounded-square macOS utility icon.
- Dark graphite/slate background.
- Activity waveform for monitoring.
- Small red power accent for deliberate stopping.
- No VPN, shield, globe, or corporate-security imagery.

The direction study is stored at
`Design/TaskScope-logo-concept.png` with its embedded C2PA Content Credentials.
It is reference material, not the shipped icon and not a product screenshot.
The app bundle icon is generated by `script/render_icon.swift` during packaging.

About TaskScope, onboarding, documentation, and release artwork should use the
same waveform/activity/power visual language as the generated bundle icon.
Avoid presenting a generic waveform-only tile as the final product mark.

## Release assets

Before publishing a downloadable binary, capture screenshots from the exact
verified release build. Use synthetic process and preset data that contains no
usernames, organization names, private commands, paths, or managed-service
details. At minimum capture:

- the main process view;
- watched-app status and a lifecycle result;
- Settings, including the permission states;
- light and dark appearance where layout or contrast differs.

Do not claim a screenshot represents macOS or architecture combinations that
were not exercised. Preserve the original generated icon source and its
provenance; do not imply that the direction study itself ships in the app.
