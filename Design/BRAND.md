# TaskScope Brand Direction

## Name

TaskScope

The name is broad enough for a menu bar process monitor while still feeling focused and utility-like. It avoids tying the app to GlobalProtect or any single process group.

## Logo Direction

- Rounded square macOS utility icon
- Dark graphite/slate background
- Activity waveform for monitoring
- Small power accent for controlled stopping
- No VPN, shield, globe, or corporate security imagery

The generated concept image is saved at `Design/TaskScope-logo-concept.png`.
The shipped app bundle icon is generated by `script/render_icon.swift` during `script/build_and_run.sh`.
