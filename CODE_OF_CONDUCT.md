# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people of different
backgrounds and experience levels, discuss ideas rather than individuals, and
assume good intent while accepting correction.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.
Do not publish another person's private information without explicit
permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and focused on
maintaining a safe project community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do
not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.
