# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people of different
backgrounds and experience levels, discuss ideas rather than individuals, and
assume good intent while accepting correction.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.
Do not publish another person's private information without explicit
permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in the repository and in project spaces or communications
where someone is representing TaskScope. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates these standards. Enforcement should be proportionate,
consistent, private where possible, and focused on community safety.

Do not open a public issue containing sensitive conduct information. A
confidential conduct-reporting address has not yet been published; maintainers
must add and verify one before inviting a public contributor community. Until
then, do not send sensitive reports through an unspecified GitHub profile or
other channel that does not explicitly promise confidentiality.

When a private channel is established, the maintainer handling a report must
acknowledge any conflict of interest and transfer the report to an uninvolved
maintainer where possible. The reporter and affected people should not be
identified publicly without consent.
